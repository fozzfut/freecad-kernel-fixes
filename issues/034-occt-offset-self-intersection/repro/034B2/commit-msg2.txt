034 B2: rim intersection hardening (rational surfaces, removed faces only, no exceptions, compound input)

- surfaces are extended in place only when the vanishing set reaches a
  removed face (the rim lemma needs no extension otherwise), and only
  polynomial B-spline / Bezier ones (GeomLib::ExtendSurfByLength keeps
  their parametrisation; not guaranteed for rational ones);
- an exception inside the rim intersection is reported as
  CannotTrimEdges (the code the stage A guard gave these offsets);
- the positive-volume check of an offset built around vanishing faces also
  covers a solid given inside a compound (FreeCAD's Part::Offset input):
  the result is then a closed shell, not a solid.
