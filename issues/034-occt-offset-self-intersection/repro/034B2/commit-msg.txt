034 B2 (TKOffset): offsets past the blend radius - vanishing faces, rim faces meet

Class: a face whose smallest principal offset factor 1 - d*k_i is below
zero (or zero within the tolerance) over the whole face - a fillet, round,
torus ring or corner ball smaller than the offset, analytic or free-form.
Its offset lies strictly closer than |d| to the input (ball argument), so
it contributes nothing to the offset.

Stock drops analytic faces in this state (status Reversed/Degenerated) but
never joins their neighbours (open shell: error or empty result) and keeps
free-form ones untrimmed (self-intersecting result). Now, in the arc join:
- such faces are found by curvature (analytic status, or a sampled factor
  with local ascent and early exit for free-form faces) and dropped;
- the offset edges they shared with their neighbours are not used;
- every pair of surviving faces around a connected vanishing set is
  intersected on copies enlarged by 10 x tolerance (the rim offsets meet
  inside their own domains; exactly on the boundary when the offset equals
  the radius); a bounded B-spline rim surface is extended in place first
  (same parametrisation, pcurves kept);
- a set that reaches a removed face of a thick solid: the rim offsets are
  extended across the set and intersected with the removed face's surface;
  ContextIntByArc skips cap edges/faces whose offset vanished (stock read an
  unbound image there);
- an offset of a solid built this way must give solids of positive volume
  (collapse beyond the half thickness -> CannotTrimEdges).
Scope: sets bounded by tangent edges or removed faces; other sets (a blend
ending on a sharp edge, whose tube folds too; partial folds inside a face)
keep the stock flow and the stage A guard. Intersection join unchanged.
