==== Lane B2 round 1, continuation 28.09.2026 (sharp rim edges, removed faces, planar-substitute orientation) ====
Pass done before the change (web search 28.09 + the lane's sources). Venues: CAD / CAGD / SMA (ACM solid modeling, conference).
B2-6. Rossignac J.R., Requicha A.A.G. Offsetting operations in solid modelling. Computer Aided Geometric Design 3(2):129-148,
      1986. doi 10.1016/0167-8396(86)90017-8. Venue: CAGD (specialist). [ABS]
      Idea: offsets as Minkowski growing/shrinking; rounding a solid = opening/closing, so an offset larger than the
      rounding radius does not see the rounding.
      Applied: the correctness criterion of this class and the independent references: the inward offset / shell of a
      box whose edges are rounded with r < d equals that of the sharp box (sharp boxes built by booleans in the tests).
B2-7. Forsyth M. Shelling and offsetting bodies. Proc. 3rd ACM Symposium on Solid Modeling and Applications (SMA) 1995,
      pp. 373-381. doi 10.1145/218013.218088 (web-checked 28.09). Venue: SMA (conference, specialist). [ABS]
      Idea: offset faces may vanish; the surviving neighbours are extended and intersected.
      Applied: already in round 1; extended here to neighbours across sharp edges whose offsets intersect.
B2-8. Lee S.H. Offsetting operations on non-manifold topological models. Computer-Aided Design 41(11):830-846, 2009.
      ScienceDirect pii S0010448509001523 (DOI not re-checked). Venue: CAD. [ABS]
      Idea: every offset element is generated by one input element (face, edge, vertex); the kind of the element and
      of its adjacency decides which offset elements meet.
      Applied: the scope rule by edge kind: a sharp edge of intersection kind generates no offset element (its faces'
      offsets meet directly), a sharp edge of tube kind generates a pipe that itself folds with the vanishing set.
B2-9. Hong Q.Y., Park Y., Kim M.-S., Elber G. Trimming offset surface self-intersections around near-singular
      regions. Computers & Graphics 82:84-94, 2019. ScienceDirect pii S0097849319300779 (DOI not re-checked). Venue: C&G (supporting, not in the
      specialist list). [ABS]
      Idea: miter points (open ends of self-intersection curves) as small parameter quads.
      Applied: design of round 2 (the tube of a sharp edge ending on a vanishing blend folds; its fold curve can
      collapse to one point - see own derivation (6)).
Own derivations (recorded here and in the commit/code comments):
  (4) Sharp-rim lemma. Let e be a sharp edge between a vanishing face Z and a rim face R whose two offsets intersect
      (convex edge, inward offset; concave edge, outward offset). The normal cone of e points into the material side
      (no tube), so no point of the offset has its nearest point on e; with (2) every boundary point of the dilation
      near Z has its nearest point inside a rim face: the pairwise intersection of the rim offsets is enough, as for
      tangent rims. Check on the box corner (fillet r, inward d >= r): the corner point (d,d) is at distance
      sqrt2 (d-r) + r >= d from the removed corner zone (equality only at d = r), so the inner corner is sharp and
      exact (erosion of the rounded box = erosion of the sharp box, B2-6).
  (5) Thick solid: the offset edge of a rim face on a removed face ends where the vanishing set began (the offset
      of the old tangent/sharp vertex), while the rim faces meet the removed face and each other at the new corner,
      which lies beyond that end for d > r and exactly at it for d = r. So the removed face must be intersected with
      the rim face extended past that end (enlarged copies), not with the stock edge.
  (6) Tube of a sharp end edge (round 2): a circular end arc of radius r of a concave blend, outward offset d > r:
      the pipe of radius d around it folds where d * sin(phi) / r > 1 (phi = angle from the side face normal); the
      valid band phi < asin(r/d) closes to one point (the arc's axis at depth sqrt(d^2 - r^2)); beyond it the pipes of
      the two straight side edges meet along x-a = z-a = sqrt(d^2 - s^2). Exact, but needs pipe trimming at the fold
      curve and pipe-pipe rim pairs; not in this round.
  (7) Planar substitution (BRepOffset_MakeOffset, stock) replaces a planar B-spline face by a plane face made FORWARD
      and loses the orientation of a REVERSED face; the removed face's copy used for the rim-pair section therefore
      takes the orientation of the face it replaced (measured: all-NURBS boxes with the bottom removed were an error
      before, exact after; analytic results unchanged).
Better-known method check: no published kernel method for this sub-class beyond Forsyth's neighbour intersection was
found; the exact partial-fold trimming (Aomura-Uehara 1990, Maekawa 1999, Hong 2019, Park 2021) stays the method for
rounds 2+.
