L1. R.T. Farouki, "The approximation of non-degenerate offset surfaces", CAGD 3(1) 15-43, 1986,
    doi:10.1016/0167-8396(86)90022-1. CAGD (specialist, full source). [ABS]
    Idea: an offset is defined by the unit normal N = Su x Sv / |Su x Sv|; regularity (Su x Sv != 0) and the
    non-degeneracy bound are intrinsic properties of the surface, i.e. invariant under rigid motions.
    APPLIED: CheckInputData's per-point test (|Su|,|Sv| > 0, Su not parallel to Sv, singular points only at
    degenerated edges) is such an intrinsic test, so it must give the same verdict in any frame; the defect
    was comparing points of two different frames (surface frame vs located frame), not the test itself.
L2. T. Maekawa, "An overview of offset curves and surfaces", CAD 31(3) 165-173, 1999,
    doi:10.1016/S0010-4485(99)00013-5. CAD (specialist, full source). [ABS]
    Idea: singular points of the parametrisation (poles, apices) need special treatment; the offset of an
    apex is a point/circle built from the surface itself.
    APPLIED: BRepOffset_Offset builds the offset apex from TheSurf; it must be placed like every other point
    of TheSurf (same frame), which the fix enforces.
Better known method? None needed: the fix is frame consistency (one frame for all compared/constructed points).
A frame-INDEPENDENT offset (none == geo, found broken on 6 members: stock) is a separate class, see README.
