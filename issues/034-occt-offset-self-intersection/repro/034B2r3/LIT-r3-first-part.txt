Issue 034 lane B2, round 3 (28.09.2026) - literature and provenance of the round-3 design elements.
(Venue rule: specialist CAD/CAGD journals count as full sources. OA = open access text read; ABS = abstract/known result.)

A. The class and why the folded part is never on the offset
 1. T. Maekawa, "An overview of offset curves and surfaces", CAD 31(3) 1999 165-173, doi 10.1016/S0010-4485(99)00013-5.
    Offset factors 1 - d k_i; a factor < 0 puts the offset point closer than |d| to nearby points of the surface.
    APPLIED: the fold samples (same criterion as the stage A guard) and the statement that no folded point is kept.
 2. N.M. Patrikalakis, T. Maekawa, "Shape Interrogation for Computer Aided Design and Manufacturing", Springer 2002,
    ch. 11 (offsets, self-intersections of offsets). OA (MIT hyperbook). APPLIED: vocabulary (cuspidal edge,
    swallowtail), the structure local self-intersection = two regular sheets crossing near a fold.

B. The self-intersection curve X and its ends (review r2 finding 1 - the core of the class)
 3. S. Aomura, T. Uehara, "Self-intersection of an offset surface", CAD 22(7) 1990 417-421. ABS (web-checked).
    Tracing the self-intersection curve of an offset surface. APPLIED: X is traced as a curve (not sampled).
 4. T. Maekawa, W. Cho, N.M. Patrikalakis, "Computation of self-intersections of offsets of Bezier surface patches",
    ASME J. Mech. Design 119(2) 1997 275-283 (supporting venue). ABS (web-checked). Starting points of the tracing
    from a system of nonlinear equations, then tracing. APPLIED: seed on the fold cross-section, then tracing.
 5. Y. Park, Q Y. Hong, M.-S. Kim, G. Elber, "Self-intersection computation for freeform surfaces based on a
    regional representation scheme for miter points", CAGD 2021 (S0167839621000248). OA preprint read
    (r3/lit/park2021.pdf/txt). Miter points are the open ends of a self-intersection curve, where its two
    parameter-space branches (u,v) and (s,t) join; near them (u,v) and (s,t) are arbitrarily close, which makes the
    plain system S(u,v) = S(s,t) singular; the xyz location of a miter point is stable, its uv location is not.
    APPLIED (own formulation): the divided-difference system G(c,th,s) = (O(c + s w) - O(c - s w)) / (2 s) = 0 is
    regular at p = q (its s = 0 branch is the fold curve with w the null direction); X reaches s = 0 exactly at
    the miter points, where the tracing stops and the end is extrapolated in s^2 (the branch is regular in s^2).
    The two preimage curves C1 = {c + s w}, C2 = {c - s w} meet at the miter point (Park et al. Fig. 1c).
 6. Q Y. Hong, Y. Park, M.-S. Kim, G. Elber, "Trimming offset surface self-intersections around near-singular
    regions", Computers & Graphics 82 2019 84-94, doi 10.1016/j.cag.2019.05.016 (C&G, supporting). ABS.
    Offset trimming regions in the parameter space; numerical care around miter points. NOT applied for region
    finding (their trimming regions are defined by distance to the surface - EP4530910A1 red flag, IP.txt);
    applied only as the observation that the trimmed region in the parameter space is bounded by the two
    preimage curves of X.
 7. E.L. Allgower, K. Georg, "Numerical Continuation Methods", Springer 1990 (book). APPLIED: pseudo-arclength
    predictor-corrector with a tangent from the generalised cross product of the 3x4 Jacobian, step control by
    the corrector iteration count and the turning angle.
 8. G. Elber, T. Grandine, M.-S. Kim, "Surface self-intersection computation via algebraic decomposition", CAD
    41(12) 2009 1060-1069. ABS. Supporting: the trivial diagonal solutions are removed by a divided difference,
    the same device as (5) in our parametrisation.
 9. D. Pekerman, G. Elber, M.-S. Kim, "Self-intersection detection and elimination in freeform curves and
    surfaces", CAD 40(2) 2008 150-159. ABS. Supporting (elimination of the self-intersecting part of a curve/surface).
10. J.-K. Seong, G. Elber, M.-S. Kim, "Trimming local and global self-intersections in offset curves/surfaces using
    distance maps", CAD 38 2006 183-193. ABS. NOT applied (distance maps; IP.txt).

C. Building the trimmed offset in the B-rep (own derivation on top of rounds 1-2)
11. Own derivation: the INPUT face is split along C1 and C2 (the lens between them holds every fold sample, checked);
    the lens is a face whose offset is trimmed away (round-1 vanishing machinery: its offset is not stored, the
    edges it shares with its neighbours bound nothing); the pieces on both sides share the offset of C1 and of
    C2, which is ONE curve X (O(C1(t)) = O(C2(t)) by construction, same parameter t = arc length of X): the
    offset of C1 is created once and bound to C2 (BRepOffset_Offset "Created" map). A lens that does not cut the
    face in two gets a straight parametric line from its miter point away from the lens to the face boundary (the
    lens opens towards c'(s^2); the line runs in the regular region, checked by the curvature factor).
    Where X leaves the face, the leaving preimage is moved onto the boundary edge exactly (3x3 Newton on
    O(pcurve_E(t)) = O(q)), the other one stays inside and a chord closes the lens; the part of X beyond the
    neighbour's section is removed by the stock loops (Inter2d).
12. Rossignac-Requicha CAGD 3 1986 and Forsyth SMA 1995 (as rounds 1-2): faces vanish, neighbours are intersected.
13. OCCT tools reused: BOPAlgo_BuilderFace (areas of a face split by edges), BRepTools_ReShape (replacement of
    faces/edges in the shapes), Geom2dAPI_Interpolate (pcurves through the traced points), BRepLib::BuildCurve3d.

D. Soundness items of the review
14. C.-Y. Hu, N.M. Patrikalakis, X. Ye, "Robust interval solid modelling part I: representations", CAD 28(10) 1996
    807-817; C.-Y. Hu, T. Maekawa, N.M. Patrikalakis, X. Ye, "Robust interval algorithm for surface
    intersections", CAD 29(9) 1997 617-627. ABS. APPLIED (finding 6): rounded interval arithmetic - every interval
    bound is moved one ulp outward (std::nextafter), the net values carry an error bound from their operation
    count (Bezier extraction, centring, rotation, de Casteljau), the hodograph hulls and the Cartesian box are
    widened by it. The 1e-10 margin is kept on top (conservative only).
15. BOPTools_AlgoTools::ComputeTolerance (the measure of the BOP curve-on-surface check): APPLIED (finding 5): the
    edges of results built around vanishing faces or lenses get the measured deviation of their pcurves as
    tolerance (it was under-reported on prolonged sections).

E. Patent position (EP4530910A1 design-around, IP.txt): the kernel decides WHAT to trim from the curvature factor
   (fold samples) and from the traced self-intersection curve X (an intersection of the offset with itself); no
   distance between the offset and the input is evaluated in the kernel. The distance check stays the stage-A
   error-raising control of the result only, and the test stand's distance oracle is test side.
