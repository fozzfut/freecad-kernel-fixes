034 B2 r3 (TKOffset): partial folds trimmed along the self-intersection curve, certified, no folded point kept

Class: free-form faces whose offset folds on a part of them only (a principal
factor 1 - d*k < 0 on a region inside the face or across it: elliptic and
B-spline profile walls, ridges and bumps of B-spline surfaces).

- Pass order: pass 1 is the flow of rounds 1-2; only when it fails and a face
  folds partially, pass 2 repeats the operation on the input split along the
  lenses of those faces. The complete state of pass 1 (error, bad shape,
  history) is put back when pass 2 fails too; a result of pass 1 is never
  changed.
- Pass 2: the self-intersection curve X is traced with the divided difference
  (O(c + s w) - O(c - s w)) / 2s (pseudo-arclength predictor-corrector), ends
  at miter points (s -> 0) or at the face boundary; the preimage curves get
  one parameter (arc length in the tracing variables, smooth through the
  miter points) and are refined until their offsets coincide within 1e-9 of
  the face size; the input is split before anything else (lens faces vanish,
  the pieces share the offset edge X), the thick-solid skin keeps the input
  faces whole, the history of the input faces/edges is remapped.
- Certificate: interval bounds of both principal factors (rounded interval
  arithmetic on Bezier patches) prove every piece that is not a lens regular,
  except the miter regions; a refuted certificate keeps the pass-1 error.
- Folded-point check of every result: a folded parameter of a face classified
  inside one of its image faces proves the result wrong (Maekawa 1999): the
  pass fails (delivered results that kept a fold - a bump, a thin rod of a
  thick solid - are now exact or an error).
- Stage A detection certified for free-form faces with an exact Bezier form
  (narrow folds between the samples); fold seeds by pattern search.
- Prolonged rim sections end at computed corners (GeomAPI_IntCS with the
  removed faces and the other rim offsets) instead of an estimated reach.
- BRepOffset_Tool: periodic re-alignment of glued pcurves, non-joining pieces
  reported and exceptions of the same-parameter step caught, all only while
  the offset is built around vanishing faces or lenses.
