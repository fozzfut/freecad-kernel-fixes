Base: r3/LIT-r3.txt (items 1-15, written by the previous agent of this round; kept). This file adds what the
continuation designed and records how every design element is sourced. Venue rule: specialist CAD/CAGD journals count
as full sources; OA = open-access text read, ABS = abstract / known result.

A. Sources re-checked for the continuation
 A1. Park, Hong, Kim, Elber, CAGD 2021 (S0167839621000248), OA preprint in r3/lit/park2021.pdf (read): miter points
     are where the two preimage branches of the self-intersection curve join; near them the plain system is
     singular; a region around each miter point is handled by itself ("regional representation"). APPLIED as:
     (i) the tracing variables (c, th, s) with the divided difference (r3 item 5); (ii) the edge parameter of X is the
     arc length of the traced curve in (c, th, s) - both preimages are smooth in it through the miter point, where s
     passes 0 linearly (own derivation: G is even in s, the branch is regular in s^2, so c(s^2), th(s^2), s is a
     regular curve in s); (iii) the certificate accepts unresolved boxes only in a neighbourhood of each miter point
     whose radius tends to 0 with the box size (the "miter region"), D-B2r3-4.
 A2. Hu, Patrikalakis, Ye, CAD 28(10) 1996; Hu, Maekawa, Patrikalakis, Ye, CAD 29(9) 1997 (ABS): rounded interval
     arithmetic (r3 item 14) - REUSED for the certificate (both principal factors bounded away from 0 on a Bezier
     patch box: trace and determinant of sigma I - dW positive).
 A3. Maekawa, CAD 31(3) 1999 (r3 item 1): a point with a negative principal factor is closer than |d| to nearby
     points of the face, hence never on the offset. APPLIED twice: (i) the certificate (the kept pieces must have
     positive factors); (ii) the folded-point check of every result (a folded parameter classified inside an image
     face proves the result wrong).
 A4. Allgower-Georg 1990 (r3 item 7): pseudo-arclength continuation; the refinement adds exact points with the same
     corrector on the hyperplane through the middle of a chord.
 A5. Elber, Grandine, Kim, CAD 41(12) 2009 (ABS) and ACM TOG 2025 doi 10.1145/3727620 (ABS, "Fast determination and
     computation of self-intersections for NURBS surfaces"): both isolate self-intersection regions of freeform
     surfaces with bounding-volume / osculating-patch tests before solving. Compared: our certificate plays the
     analogous role for the OFFSET folds (a regular region is proven, the rest must be inside a lens or a miter
     region). A better-known complete method for GLOBAL self-intersections (TOG 2025) is not implemented (out of
     this round, recorded as remaining).
 A6. Rossignac-Requicha CAGD 1986 (erosion = complement of dilation of the complement): used only on the TEST side for
     the exact references of prism members (ref/ellref.py, ref/profref.py: disc under the graph, dilation = product of
     the 2D dilation and the ball in the third axis).

B. Own derivations of the continuation (no external source)
 B1. Stock-first pass order with state snapshot (OffsetState034c): pass 1 = the flow of rounds 1-2, pass 2 only after
     pass 1 fails; a failed pass 2 puts the complete state of pass 1 back (error, bad shape, history).
 B2. Split-first: the lens split replaces the input before the planar substitution / analysis (the planar faces
     next to a split face then follow the split); history of the input faces and edges remapped after pass 2
     (face substitution map for faces whose edges were split; images of the pieces for split faces and edges).
 B3. Thick-solid skin: the kept input faces are glued whole (edge splits only), not the pieces.
 B4. Adaptive refinement of the lens preimage curves: interpolate both with the common parameter, measure the
     distance of the offsets of the two interpolants in the middle of every interval, insert exact points until it is
     below 1e-9 of the face size (near a miter point, where the corrector is singular, 20x that is accepted and kept
     as edge tolerance).
 B5. Certificate on the pieces (exact B-rep domains): Bezier patches of the surface (exact B-spline form of
     extrusions/revolutions of B-spline curves and conics, parameters checked), boxes outside the piece skipped
     (distance of the box middle to the boundary polylines with their chord sagitta, then the 2D classifier),
     boxes proven regular, others cut; unresolved boxes only in the miter regions.
 B6. Folded-point check of every result (pass 1 included): folded grid samples of a face classified in its image
     faces (same parameters when the image surface is the offset of the face surface in its parameters - checked on
     three points; otherwise the offset point is projected: an error-raising control).
 B7. Exact corners for the prolonged rim sections (review r2 MAJOR "reach"): the curve that carries the section is
     intersected with the removed faces of the set and with the offsets of the other rim faces (GeomAPI_IntCS); the
     section is prolonged to the nearest such corner beyond its end plus a crossing margin (1e-3 |d| + 10 tol,
     numerical only); no corner in the curve domain -> not prolonged (open loop -> error). The arc-length reach
     "set diagonal + |d|" is no longer used for prolongation; it remains only as the enlargement of the copies of rim
     pairs WITHOUT an edge on the removed face and of the rim boxes for culling (an estimate, not a bound: a copy that
     does not reach the corner leaves the loop open -> error, never silent; recorded as remaining).

C. IP (EP4530910A1 design-around, issue 034 IP.txt): the continuation adds no distance evaluation that decides WHAT
   is built or trimmed: the certificate and the folded-point check use curvature (principal factors) and parameters;
   the only distance-like step (projection of an offset point on an image surface of another parametrisation) is in
   the folded-point CHECK, which can only raise an error. The exact corners are curve/surface intersections of the
   geometry being built (as the stock BRepOffset intersections). Verdict unchanged: no obstacle found for RU/EAEU;
   attorney check before any EU/US release.
